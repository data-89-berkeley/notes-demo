Surfaces, Level Sets & Gradients

Interactive companions for Chapter 8. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, double-click to reset the view.

Surface cross-section and one-variable linearization. Fix one coordinate, and the surface becomes a curve in the other. The dashed lines are the tangent lines to those curves at the point. Their slopes are the partial derivatives.

Level sets. Slice the surface with a horizontal plane at height z. The red curve where they meet is the level set {(x, y) : f(x, y) = z}. The floor shows it projected onto the xy-plane, alongside a topographic map of other levels.

Gradient field. The blue arrows on the floor point along ∇f, the direction of steepest ascent. At (x₀, y₀), the red arrow is ∇f. Turn on Show contour to draw the level set through the point in red: the gradient is always perpendicular to it.
